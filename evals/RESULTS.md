# Evaluation findings

A November 6, 2026 pilot compared three identical two-icon briefs with the skill disabled, enabled, and revised: structural reconstruction, organic reconstruction, and new audio subjects. Each used a fresh isolated Codex process, the same supplied images, GPT-6-Astra with high reasoning, and the same output contract. Nine initial runs completed, plus one targeted audio rerun after an ambiguity-check refinement. This is exploratory evidence, not a general performance claim.

## What the evidence supports

- Runtime probes distinguished baseline skill absence from treatment availability. Command traces show the skill read in all six initial treatment runs and no skill reads in the three baselines. Six routing probes behaved as expected: three icon requests loaded it; three unrelated requests did not.
- All 18 initial SVGs passed inventory, XML, native-frame, and dimension checks. Desktop and mobile comparison views rendered without horizontal overflow. An independent reviewer inspected anonymized outputs and actual SVGs.
- Structural reproduction was effectively tied. The revised workflow and baseline had small organic-contour advantages over the initial enabled workflow. The revised recorder was economical; audio tape meaning remained ambiguous. The targeted rerun changed the ambiguity rather than eliminating it. No overall winner or perfect-result claim is justified.

The baseline also performed well when given clean crops and a precise brief. Better inputs and direct rendered comparison matter at least as much as additional prose. The current workflow therefore makes reference choice, topology, terminal shapes, and a second visual pass explicit, and permits faithful reproduction instead of requiring novelty.

## Limits

One run per initial case/treatment cannot establish reliability or isolate all causes. Raster masks and overlap scores depend on threshold, resolution, and alignment; they do not prove original-vector equality. The CLI trace format did not expose image-view events, so individual run claims of visual inspection are self-reports. Separate review of the saved sheets and browser comparisons was performed.

Unfamiliar subjects may still require a label or a dedicated compact drawing. Exact reuse of a supplied SVG is a different task from reconstructing a screenshot. Human visual preference, application integration, and accessibility of future controls were not certified. Private source images and raw run traces are deliberately excluded from this package.

See [the evaluation protocol](README.md) to repeat the comparison with your own inputs. Method informed by [Evaluating skills](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills).
